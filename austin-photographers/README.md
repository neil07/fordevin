# Austin-area photographers (for hiring or collaboration)

- `austin_photographers.xlsx`: three sheets. **All** has 111 businesses. **Austin offices** has the 24 with an office. **Summary** counts them by segment and address type.
- `austin_photographers.csv`: the same data as plain CSV.

## Main findings
1. **24 of 111 businesses (22%) have a commercial office or studio in Austin.** Most are headshot, product and portrait/newborn studios, which need a fixed space to shoot in.
2. **Real estate and architecture photographers almost never have an office.** Only 4 of 56 do: Come and Shoot It (Cedar Park), Boko Media, Justin Wallace and Thomas McConnell. For these two segments an office says little about how reliable the business is. Portfolio, turnaround time and reviews are better signals.
3. **Justin Wallace Architecture Photography and Photogroup Studios share one address** (321 W Ben White #106A). They are probably the same business or share a studio.

## Accuracy
- All data comes from search-engine results and directory listings (Yelp, BBB, The Knot, Houzz, etc.). The businesses' own websites were not opened directly. **Confirm phone numbers and addresses on each site before reaching out.**
- "Address listed (type unclear)" means a street address is published but the building type is unknown. Many of these are probably homes.
- Addresses that conflict between sources are recorded in the Notes column (for example Torrid Boudoir and JPM Real Estate Photography).
